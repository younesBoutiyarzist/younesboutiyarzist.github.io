Design, assembly, and integration of a complete **autonomous UAV platform**, from the airframe and embedded electronics to flight-control software and mission execution.

The system integrates a **Cube flight controller**, GNSS/IMU sensors, and onboard electronics with **ArduPilot** for flight control and autonomous navigation.

**Mission Planner** is used for system configuration, sensor calibration, flight monitoring, and autonomous mission definition.

The project provides hands-on experience with the complete robotics stack, including **hardware integration, embedded sensing, flight-control systems, communication interfaces, system calibration, and real-world flight validation**.
