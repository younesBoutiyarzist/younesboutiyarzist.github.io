Targetless calibration of a heterogeneous multi-sensor platform combining **IMU, stereo fisheye cameras, and radar**, without requiring dedicated calibration targets.

The objective is to estimate the spatial relationships between the different sensor reference frames to enable consistent multi-sensor fusion and perception.
