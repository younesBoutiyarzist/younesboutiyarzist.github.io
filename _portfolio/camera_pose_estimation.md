Development of vision-based methods for **6-DoF camera pose estimation and UAV localization** from 2D image observations and known 3D geometric structures.

The work focuses on robust **2D–3D correspondence estimation**, probabilistic data association, and nonlinear pose optimization to recover the camera position and orientation under uncertainty, outliers, and partial observations.

The methods are evaluated on UAV flight experiments using structured environments such as **airport markings and helipads**, with the objective of enabling reliable localization when GNSS information is unavailable or degraded.
